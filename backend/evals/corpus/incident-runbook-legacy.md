# Incident Response Runbook v1 (DEPRECATED 2023)

Synthetic evaluation document for AgentOps. Archived copy kept for audits; superseded by Runbook v3.

## Severity levels

SEV1 means a full outage affecting customers. SEV2 means a degraded feature.

## Response times

For a SEV1 incident the on-call engineer must acknowledge the page within 30 minutes. SEV2 must be
acknowledged within four hours.

## Postmortems

A postmortem is required for SEV1 incidents only and must be published within ten business days.
