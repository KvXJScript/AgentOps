# Remote Work Policy

Synthetic evaluation document for AgentOps. Owner: People Team. Version 2.1.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their three-month probation period may work remotely up to four days per week.
Team leads can approve a fully remote arrangement for roles that do not require on-site hardware access.

## Equipment allowance

Remote employees receive a one-time home office allowance of 1,200 EUR for a desk, a chair and a monitor.
Laptops are provided by IT and remain company property. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of purchase.

## Core hours

Everyone must be reachable on chat and video between 10:00 and 15:00 in their local time zone.
Outside core hours people organise their own schedule, as long as meetings are announced one day in advance.

## Security

Company systems may only be accessed through the corporate VPN. Working from public Wi-Fi without the VPN is not
allowed. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 24 hours.
